## Minimalist Blogging websites using HTML and CSS
*Nov 20, 2025,  | Category: Design*

I feel that bypassing bulky CMS systems leads to faster sites and better SEO.

This is the core reason for my **from-scratch approach** to building this blog. No frameworks, no bloat—just pure HTML and CSS.

When you control the code, you control the narrative. That's what minimalism truly means. Minimalism forces the mind to find the most efficient and crispy way to represent or share information with no complexities. This is the main advantage of having a minimalist approach for information interchange. 

Lastly, the effectiveness of information intake depends on the ease of understanding what is to be understood with minimum distractions. 

Happy minimalism.
